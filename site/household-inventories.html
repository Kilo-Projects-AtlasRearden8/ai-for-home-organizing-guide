Household Inventories

Knowing what you own saves money, helps with insurance, and makes moving or decluttering much easier. AI helps you list it without writing everything by hand.

What to inventory

You do not need to inventory every sock. Start with categories that matter:

  • Electronics — computers, phones, tablets, game consoles, cameras
  • Appliances — microwave, vacuum, washer, dryer, small kitchen appliances
  • Furniture — beds, sofas, tables, chairs, dressers
  • Clothing — coats, shoes, formal wear (group the rest by season)
  • Tools and gear — drills, bikes, camping, sports equipment
  • Media — books, games, movies you care about

How to build your list

Option 1: Room by room with AI

Go into one room with your phone. Take notes or photos of the big items. Then tell AI:

“I’m making a home inventory. Here is what is in my living room: a sofa, a TV, a coffee table, two lamps. Suggest a simple table format with columns for Item, Room, Approximate Value, and Notes.”

Copy the table into a spreadsheet or a note app.

Option 2: Photo + AI description

Photograph each valuable item (see Photo Routines). Ask AI to describe the photo and suggest an inventory entry:

“This is a photo of my laptop. Write a one-line inventory entry: brand, approximate age, condition.”

You can ask AI to write the formulas or set up the sheet for you.

Keep it current

Add new major purchases as you make them. Once a year, walk through your home and update the list. A fifteen-minute annual walkthrough beats trying to remember everything after a fire or move.

Store it safely

Keep your inventory in more than one place:

  • A spreadsheet on your computer
  • A printed copy in a folder with your important papers
  • A photo of the spreadsheet on your phone (no account numbers)

Insurance tip

If you have homeowners or renters insurance, ask your agent what they need for a claim. A photo and a rough value is usually enough for everyday items; keep receipts for anything expensive.

Privacy reminder: do not include serial numbers combined with personal account information in an inventory you share with AI. Serial numbers alone are generally fine, but keep the full list private to your own devices.
