# Privacy First

Before you do anything else with AI at home, read this page. It takes two minutes, and it will keep your family safe.

## The golden rule

**Never scan or upload sensitive IDs to an AI.**

That means passports, driver's licenses, state IDs, Social Security cards,
birth certificates, marriage certificates, and anything with your Social
Security number on it. If a document can be used to steal your identity, do
not feed it to an AI chatbot — not even one that promises it is private.

## Why

AI companies may keep your uploads to train their models, and even careful
companies can be breached. Once your ID information is in a system you do not
control, you cannot take it back. Identity theft is hard to undo. A little
caution now saves a lot of trouble later.

## What IS safe to share

You can generally share with AI:

- Grocery lists and meal plans
- Family schedules and school calendars (names are fine, but leave off phone
  numbers and addresses if you want to be extra careful)
- Photos of household items for inventory (make sure no IDs are visible in
  the background)
- Your own writing, recipes, and chore routines
- General questions about how to organize something

## A good habit: the "stranger test"

Before you paste or upload anything, ask: "Would I show this to a stranger
standing in line at the grocery store?" If the answer is no, do not share it
with AI either.

## Local-first is better

Whenever possible, use tools that run on your own computer instead of sending
your data to a website. This manual points you toward local options where they
exist, but even with local tools, keep the golden rule: no sensitive IDs.
