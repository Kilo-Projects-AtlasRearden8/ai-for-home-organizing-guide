# Photo Routines

Phone photos pile up fast. AI can help you sort, find, and enjoy them without
spending a whole weekend staring at a screen.

## The problem

Most of us have thousands of photos: kids, pets, meals, receipts, screenshots,
and that one blurry picture of a ceiling. AI is good at spotting what is in a
picture, which makes it useful for organizing — but you still make the
decisions.

## Routine A: The weekly 10-minute cleanup

Do this once a week, preferably on the same day:

1. Open your phone's "recent" photos.
2. Delete the obvious junk: blurry shots, duplicate screenshots, photos of
   signs you already remembered.
3. Favorite the ones you want to keep easy access to (this week's highlights).
4. Move the rest into an album named by month (for example, "October 2026").

Set a timer for 10 minutes. When it rings, stop. A little every week beats a
marathon once a year.

## Routine B: Ask AI to describe a photo

Many AI apps let you upload a photo and ask what is in it. This is great for:

- Remembering where you took a picture
- Identifying a plant, dish, or item you want to buy again
- Writing a caption for a photo album

> "What is in this photo? Write a one-sentence caption I can save with it."

Privacy: make sure no sensitive IDs, addresses, or credit cards are visible
in the photo before you upload it.

## Routine C: Build a household photo inventory

For insurance and your own peace of mind, photograph valuable items once a
year:

- Electronics with serial numbers visible
- Furniture
- Jewelry
- Tools and hobby gear

Put the photos in an album called "Home Inventory" and note what each item is
and roughly what you paid. Do not include account numbers or IDs in the
photos.

## Routine D: Duplicates and backups

- Use your phone's built-in duplicate detector, or a free app, to remove
  exact copies.
- Back up photos to one place you control: a computer, an external drive, or
  a cloud account you own. Having photos in only one place is risky; two
  places is better.

## ADHD-friendly tip

If photo organization feels overwhelming, do one album per month, or even one
per season. Finishing a small album is a win. Skipping a week is fine — just
start again next week.

## Privacy reminder

No sensitive IDs in photos you upload to AI. Check the background of pictures
too — a driver's license on the kitchen counter counts.
