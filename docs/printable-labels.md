# Printable Labels

Labels make bins, shelves, and drawers easier to use — for you and for
everyone else in the house. This page shows you how to design labels with AI
and print them at home.

## What to label

Start small. Pick one area:

- Pantry bins
- Cleaning supplies under the sink
- Kids' toy bins
- Office drawers
- Holiday decoration boxes

Label what is inside, not just where it belongs.

## Designing labels with AI

You do not need graphic design skills. Ask AI to write label text:

> "I need labels for pantry bins. Make short, clear labels for: baking
> supplies, snacks, canned goods, breakfast, pasta and rice, oils and vinegar.
> Keep each label under three words."

AI can also suggest a labeling system:

> "Suggest a simple color-coding system for a family of four so everyone
> knows whose bin is whose."

## Making printable labels

You have two easy options:

### Option 1: Word processor

Open any document app (Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, Pages). Make a table,
put one label in each cell, print on sticker paper or regular paper and cut
it out.

### Option 2: The printable page in this manual

This page has blank label templates sized for standard sticker paper
(approximately 2" x 1" labels, 12 per page) and blank bins you can write on
by hand. Press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P) to print.

## Writing good labels

- Short: two or three words max
- Clear: use words everyone in the house knows
- Consistent: use the same word for the same thing (do not use both "snacks"
  and "food")
- Big enough to read from a few feet away

## If you have a label printer

If you own a Brother P-touch, Dymo, or similar label printer, you can type
AI's suggested label text directly into the label maker's app. No cutting
required.

## ADHD-friendly tip

Label one drawer, not the whole room. Finishing one drawer is a complete win.
Leave the rest for another day.

## Privacy reminder

Labels live in your home, so they can include personal details if you want.
Just do not print labels with account numbers, passwords, or ID numbers, and
do not upload photos of your labels to AI if they show private information.
